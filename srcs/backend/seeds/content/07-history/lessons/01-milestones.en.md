In little more than a century, we went from a 12-second hop above a beach to telescopes parked 1.5 million kilometres from the Earth. Here are the main milestones.

## The age of aviation

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 17 December 1903 | The **Wright** brothers make the first powered, controlled aeroplane flight at Kitty Hawk (USA), with the *Flyer*: 12 seconds, 37 metres. |
| 25 July 1909 | **Louis Blériot** flies across the English Channel. |
| 1927 | Charles Lindbergh flies non-stop from New York to Paris, alone. |
| 1947 | The Bell X-1, flown by Chuck Yeager, breaks the sound barrier in level flight. |
| 2 March 1969 | First flight of **Concorde**, the Franco-British supersonic airliner, in service from 1976 to 2003. |

## The space race

Modern rockets descend from the work of pioneers such as Konstantin Tsiolkovsky, Robert Goddard (first liquid-propellant rocket, 1926) and Hermann Oberth, then from the missiles of the Second World War. The rivalry between the United States and the USSR sped everything up:

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 4 October 1957 | The USSR launches **Sputnik 1**, the first artificial satellite. |
| 1958 | **NASA** is founded in the United States. |
| 12 April 1961 | **Yuri Gagarin**, aboard Vostok 1, is the first human in space. |
| 1961 | **CNES**, the French space agency, is founded. |
| 20 July 1969 | **Apollo 11**: Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walk on the Moon, Michael Collins stays in orbit. |

## The age of cooperation

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 1975 | The **European Space Agency (ESA)** is founded. |
| 24 December 1979 | First flight of **Ariane 1**, from Kourou. |
| 1982 | Jean-Loup Chrétien, the first French person in space. |
| 1990 | The **Hubble** Space Telescope reaches orbit. |
| 1998 | First module of the **International Space Station**, built by the United States, Russia, Europe, Japan and Canada. |
| 12 November 2014 | The **Philae** lander, from ESA's **Rosetta** mission, touches down on a comet. |
| 21 December 2015 | SpaceX lands the first stage of a Falcon 9 after an orbital launch, a first. |
| 25 December 2021 | An **Ariane 5** launches the **James Webb** Space Telescope from Kourou. |
| 9 July 2024 | First flight of **Ariane 6**. |
